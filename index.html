GOOD TO KNOW

Before you settle in.

What if I’m not sure what to book?+

Choose the complimentary 30-minute consultation on Molly’s booking page. It’s a chance to talk through your goals and get help choosing the right service.

Can I come in without an appointment?+

MJ Salon is by appointment only. You can see availability and reserve a time through the online booking page.

What if I need to change my appointment?+

Molly asks for 48 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Less than 48 hours can result in a 30% fee; a no-show or cancellation within two hours can result in a 75% fee. Contact Molly if life happens.

Is parking available?+

Yes. Free parking is available in the parking lot.
